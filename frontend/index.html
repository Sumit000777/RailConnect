ABOUT US

Built to make railway booking simple

RailYatra gives every traveller their own account to search trains, reserve tickets, and manage their journeys — no admin desk, no middleman. Everything you need lives in your personal dashboard.

HOW IT WORKS

From search to ticket in five steps

01

Sign up

Create an account with your email — takes less than a minute.

02

Search trains

Look up trains by station, train number, or train name.

03

Add passengers

Enter traveller details and pick a preferred berth.

04

Confirm booking

No payment step — confirm and your ticket is generated instantly.

Manage anytime

View, track or cancel your bookings from Booking History.
